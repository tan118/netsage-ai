# NET-011

## Symptom
DHCP fails after an ACL is applied to the internal interface.

## Topology
Internal clients need DHCP plus DNS/HTTP access through the router.

## Show Outputs
show access-lists -> ACL does not permit required DHCP traffic; simulation shows DHCP packet dropped at router.

## Expected Fault
ACL is blocking DHCP traffic.

## OSI Layer
Layer 3/4

## Concept
DHCP + ACL interaction

## Severity
High
