# NET-030

## Symptom
Guest laptop cannot ping its own gateway.

## Topology
Laptop belongs to visitor VLAN 60 and should reach 192.168.60.1.

## Show Outputs
show interfaces trunk -> VLAN 60 is not carried on the trunk toward the gateway.

## Expected Fault
Guest VLAN is missing from the trunk.

## OSI Layer
Layer 2

## Concept
Wireless VLAN trunking

## Severity
High
