# NET-002

## Symptom
Hosts behind two switches cannot communicate across the switch-to-switch link.

## Topology
SW1 and SW2 are connected by a trunk; both hosts use VLAN 20.

## Show Outputs
show interfaces trunk -> VLAN 20 is not in the allowed VLAN list.

## Expected Fault
VLAN 20 is missing from the trunk allowed list.

## OSI Layer
Layer 2

## Concept
802.1Q trunking

## Severity
High
